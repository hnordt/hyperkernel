# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Hyperkernel contributors are expected to communicate with care, disagree about
ideas without attacking people, respect different levels of experience, and
give actionable feedback grounded in the project’s documented scope.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces where someone is representing the Hyperkernel project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/hnordt/hyperkernel/security/advisories/new).
Use “Code of Conduct report” as the title and include links or screenshots when
available.

The maintainer will review reports as confidentially as practical and may edit
or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently exclude a participant when necessary. Retaliation against
someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
